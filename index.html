    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images and financial numbers uploaded to a server?

    No. KitMint runs entirely in your browser. Images are decoded and re-encoded with the HTML5 Canvas API, and your profit, margin and ROAS figures are calculated with JavaScript on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, so your files and numbers stay private. Close the tab and everything is gone.

    How do I compress PNG or JPG images to WebP without losing quality?

    Drop your image into the Image Optimizer, choose WebP in the Convert to menu, and set the quality slider between 75% and 85%. At those settings WebP is usually far smaller than PNG or JPEG with no visible difference. The side-by-side preview and the size comparison show the result before you download, so you can raise the quality if you spot any change.

    How is Break-Even ROAS calculated for e-commerce products?

    Break-even ROAS equals your selling price divided by your profit before ad spend. First subtract the product cost, shipping, packaging and platform fee from the selling price. For example, a 50 selling price with 30 in costs leaves 20 before ads, so break-even ROAS is 50 divided by 20, or 2.5x. If your ads return more than 2.5 in revenue for every 1 spent, each sale makes a profit.

    What is the ideal character limit for Google Title Tags and Meta Descriptions?

    Aim for about 60 characters for title tags and about 160 characters for meta descriptions. Google truncates by pixel width rather than a fixed count, so these are safe guidelines, not hard limits, and longer text may be cut off with an ellipsis in search results. The Text Formatter shows live length meters for both as you type.
